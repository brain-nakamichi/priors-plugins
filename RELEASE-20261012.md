## Behavior

- Remind the AI to save durable, important instructions, decisions, constraints, unresolved commitments, material corrections, and significant operational or safety outcomes without waiting for an explicit save request.
- Retrieve the narrowest relevant prior information before relying on it when an old decision or missing context could materially affect the answer or an important action.
- The AI chooses which Priors functions to combine; there is no all-turn search or automatic A→B→C topic-switch detector.
- Hooks and local candidate signals are prompts, not proof of a saved or retrieved memory. A save is confirmed only by the successful Priors response with its memory ID and version.
- Existing safety, ownership, authorization, source, and secret-handling constraints remain in force.
